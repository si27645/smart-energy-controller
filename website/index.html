The problem

Every device is smart. The house isn't.

// wasted surplus

The sun gives you 3 kW extra at noon and nobody's home to plug in the car — it all goes to the grid for a few cents.

// mismanaged battery

The water heater kicks in mid-afternoon with the battery at 40%, spending what should have been saved for the night.

// fragile automations

Home Assistant has all the data, but keeping the right automations running takes YAML, time, and constant trial and error.

How it works

Rules that explain themselves

The engine recalculates every few minutes, crossing forecast solar production, the dynamic electricity price, and the real state of each device. Have a battery? It optimizes charge and discharge. Don't? It uses the water heater and heat pump as thermal storage — the engine adapts to whatever exists in your home.

IF solar_surplus > 3.2 kW

→charge the electric car

IF battery.soc > 80%

→turn on the water heater

IF tomorrow_forecast = low

→don't drain the battery today

IF price@03:00 = low

→charge the battery from the grid

IF ev.needs = 20 kWh before 08:00

→optimize the charging window

IF decision = any

→always show why

IF solar_surplus > 1.5 kW (no battery)

→heat the water tank to the max — thermal battery
